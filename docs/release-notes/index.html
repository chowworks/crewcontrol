    SHIPPEDNov 1, 2026

    Organizers going on their own trip

    Hosting and going? Press "I'm going too" in Manage Guests on Trip Control. You take one of the trip's spots at once, marked Organizer, with no trip agreement to sign and no need to accept yourself.

    You start owing nothing. If you're paying your share into the trip, add a charge to your own balance in your Details, and log what you put in like any payment.

    Good to know
    • Every owner and organizer on the trip can do it, each for themselves.
    • It needs a free spot. "I'm not going" on your own row gives the spot back.
    • You get key date reminders like everyone else, but no payment reminders.

    SHIPPEDOct 31, 2026

    Initial release: Travel HQ is live

    Travel HQ v1 went live at travel-hq.app on October 31, 2026. An organizer builds one trip page with photos, highlights, key dates and a FAQ, and shares its link (or a flyer made from it) with the group. Guests tap "I want to join", sign the trip agreement and, depending on how the trip runs, are accepted first or pay first.

    Trip Control puts what needs the organizer at the top: guests to accept, payments to confirm, questions to answer. Trip Financials shows who has paid and who owes what, with exports for a spreadsheet. Guests pay the organizer directly, by Zelle, Cash App or cash, and Travel HQ keeps the record. It never moves the money.

    Good to know
    • Payment plans with installments, scheduled email reminders (guests can stop them), and forwarded payment receipts matched to the guest who paid.
    • Rooms with a picture of each, an optional price per room, a waitlist for full trips, and one person signing up and paying for travelers they look after.
    • Announcements go to accepted guests on the trip page, and they can reply.
    • An owner adds co-organizers on the People screen, and can delete a trip after downloading everything on it.
    • During the early access preview, the Travel HQ team starts new trips; anyone else can ask for early access. Text messages aren't sent yet; email is.
